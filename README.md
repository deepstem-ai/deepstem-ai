## 🔬 Featured Research & Implementation

I am actively developing "Edge AI" solutions that address specific medical and behavioral challenges.

### 🧠 Project NeuroScan: Behavioral Biomarker Analysis
*(Focus: Bioinformatics & Computer Vision)*
An automated screening tool designed to assist in the early detection of **Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)**.
* **The Problem:** ADHD diagnosis often relies on subjective observation.
* **The Solution:** A computer vision pipeline that analyzes temporal patterns in micro-movements, gaze stability, and engagement metrics during cognitive tasks.
* **Technology:** Deep Learning models deployed on **Nvidia Jetson Orin Nano** to process video feeds in real-time, ensuring patient data privacy (no cloud upload).

### 🖐️ Project SurgiGest: Sterile Human-Machine Interface
*(Focus: Biomedical Engineering & Robotics)*
A contactless control system designed for operating theaters and high-sterility labs.
* **The Problem:** Surgeons cannot touch computer peripherals (mouse/keyboard) once scrubbed in, making accessing digital imaging difficult.
* **The Solution:** A high-precision hand-tracking system that converts mid-air gestures into cursor commands.
* **Technology:** Optimized **TensorRT** models for hand pose estimation, achieving sub-100ms latency for seamless interaction.

### 👁️ Project BioFlow: Clinical Density & Vitals Monitoring
*(Focus: Computer Engineering & Medical Operations)*
A smart-monitoring system for clinical environments.
* **The Function:** Tracks patient density in waiting areas and utilizes remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) concepts to estimate heart rate variability via video.
* **The Impact:** Reduces administrative burden on nurses and provides non-contact triage data.
